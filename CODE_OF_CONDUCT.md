# Code of Conduct

AXtory contributors are expected to make participation respectful, harassment-free, and safe for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically, respect privacy, and do
not pressure anyone to disclose proprietary code, prompts, sessions, credentials, or personal data.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private
information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior is
inconsistent with these expectations. Reports should use a private maintainer channel whenever
they involve safety or personal information. Until a dedicated conduct address exists, request a
private channel through a content-free GitHub issue.

This policy is intentionally concise for the pre-release project and will be replaced by a
versioned community standard before accepting a broad contributor community.
